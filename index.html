The World of Haven

You don't fight the dark.
You bring the light.

Haven is a cooperative RPG with a different heart. You don't push back the dark by fighting it — you push it back by creating light. What's fallen can be healed, monsters can be freed, and even the ones who once stood against you can become friends.

Clearing a place of monsters never lasts — the shadow creeps back. You make somewhere truly safe by building it, together. Here, cooperation isn't a bonus. It's how you win, and the deeper you and your group go, the stronger you all become.

If you grew up raiding in EverQuest or WoW and wish you could share that feeling with your kids, your partner, or your friends across the country, Haven is built for you. Short sessions. Every platform. No toxicity by design.

How You Play

The shadow holds many secrets

Beyond the lantern-light, the world lies broken and waiting. Every ruin, every silent grove, every soul lost to the dark is hiding something worth finding. Haven is a loop you'll never want to leave — venture out, bring the light home, and gather the friends who'll carry you through what's coming.

01

Explore the Dark

Venture past the safety of the village into a fractured, beautiful world. Search its ruins and wilds for the resources, relics, and lost souls the shadow has swallowed — the deeper you go, the greater the reward.

02

Bring It Home

Carry everything you find back to the village. Rebuild it, grow it, and light it — because clearing out monsters never lasts, and the only place that stays safe is the one you build together.

03

Find Your Friends

You were never meant to do this alone. Rescue the lost, free the corrupted, and turn yesterday's enemies into today's allies. Every friend you make along the way makes your whole party stronger.

04

Face What You Can't Alone

Some secrets are guarded by things no single hero can stand against. When the dark pushes back hardest, you'll need a full party at your side — and all the trust you built getting there.

↻ Rest, resupply, and venture out again — a world that always has more to give.

Choose Your Role

Pick your part in the party

Haven's classes are built to need each other. Tank, protect, deal damage, and control the fight together — because cooperation isn't a bonus here, it's how you win.

Warrior

Tank · Vanguard

Stand at the front and hold the line. Taunt the darkness onto yourself and shield your party in light, so your friends can do their work behind you.

Mage

Damage · Elementalist

Command fire and ice from range. Freeze, burn, and shatter corruption before it ever reaches your friends — raw light turned to devastating magic.

Healer

Protector · Support

Keep your party standing when the dark pushes back. Mend wounds, raise the fallen, and wrap your friends in Shielding Light — because no one lights the dark alone.

Ranger

Control · Hunter

Master of the battlefield. Mark your prey, lay snares, and vanish into the treeline, controlling every fight from the edges. The Ranger joins Haven soon.
