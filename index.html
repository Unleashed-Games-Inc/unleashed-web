The World of Haven

You don't fight the dark. You bring the light.

Haven is a cooperative RPG with a different heart. You don't push back the dark by fighting it — you push it back by creating light. What's fallen can be healed, monsters can be freed, and even the ones who once stood against you can become friends.

Clearing a place of monsters never lasts — the shadow creeps back. You make somewhere truly safe by building it, together. Here, cooperation isn't a bonus. It's how you win, and the deeper you and your group go, the stronger you all become.

If you grew up raiding in EverQuest or WoW and wish you could share that feeling with your kids, your partner, or your friends across the country, Haven is built for you. Short sessions. Every platform. No toxicity by design.

What Makes Haven Different

Not your typical MMO

❤

Rescue the Lost

Darkness in Haven is a sickness, not a sentence. Free corrupted creatures and lost souls, and the ones you save join your story — and make your whole group stronger.

🏗

Build Something Worth Protecting

Clearing out monsters never holds — the shadow creeps back in. The only way to make a place truly safe is to build it: a home and a community that grows alongside you and your friends.

🤝

Cooperation Wins

Helping each other is the whole point — and how you win. Light the dark together across short, welcoming sessions, with no competition for loot and no toxicity by design.

📱

Every Platform, One World

PC, Mac, Switch, Xbox, PlayStation, iOS, Android. Dad's on console, the kids are on tablets, you're on PC. Same world. Same adventure. Your progress persists when you log off.
