Welcome to Athera

The world we once knew has been lost to shadow

Athera is a fractured world of Shard Isles surrounded by shifting, dangerous Shadow Lands. Explore mysterious ruins, fight corrupted creatures, rescue lost souls, and build thriving villages with your friends and family. The deeper you go together, the stronger you all become.

If you grew up raiding in EverQuest or WoW and wish you could share that feeling with your kids, your partner, or your friends across the country, Haven is built for you. Short sessions. Every platform. No toxicity by design.

Early Access 2027 · Short, session-friendly play · Family & couples pricing

What Makes Haven Different

Not your typical MMO

⚔

Rescue, Don't Kill

Corrupted enemies can be freed from the Shadow and brought back to your village. They bring skills, knowledge, and power with them. The people who fought you become the people who make you stronger.

🏗

Build Your Village

Your village is the heart of the game. Grow it from a homestead into a thriving community. Recruit NPCs, craft, build, and unlock new abilities as your village levels up alongside you.

🌍

Explore the Shadow Lands

Venture into a shifting wilderness where distance behaves strangely and forgotten places wait to be reclaimed. Procedurally generated terrain means no two expeditions are the same.

📱

Every Platform, One World

PC, Mac, Switch, Xbox, PlayStation, iOS, Android. Dad's on console, the kids are on tablets, you're on PC. Same world. Same adventure. Your progress persists when you log off.
